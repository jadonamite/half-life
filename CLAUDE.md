
## Design & UI/UX Mandate (STRICT)

**CRITICAL: Before starting ANY frontend, UI, or design task across ANY project in Inertia, you MUST automatically load and apply the following skills/guidelines:**
- `ui-ux-pro-max`
- `frontend-design`
- `design-spells`
- `landing-page-generator`
- All "Taste" and UX skills (e.g., `ux-persuasion-engineer`, `design-md`, `uxui-principles`)
- All Copywriting skills (e.g., `copywriting`, `copywriting-psychologist`)

### MANDATORY REQUIREMENTS (MUST HAVE)
- **TOC & Privacy:** You MUST ALWAYS include Terms of Service / Terms and Conditions (TOC) and Privacy Policy links/sections.
- **High-Craft Execution:** Drop shadows and liquid glass ARE ALLOWED, but they must be extremely high quality and deliberate. Do NOT make them look like generic "AI slop".
- **Authenticity:** You MUST use real product demos (no fake/empty placeholders).

### STRICT ANTI-PATTERNS (WHAT NOT TO DO)
Under NO circumstances should you include any of the following. These patterns explicitly make an app look like "AI slop":

#### Visuals & Styling
- NO hash gradients, standard gradients, or "grades"
- NO pure white backgrounds
- NO rainbow coloring
- NO moldings
- NO soft corner radius (use sharp, intentional structural radii)
- NO purple and black color schemes
- NO neon colors or basic pastel colors
- NO radial orbs
- NO colored left stripes

#### Layout & Components
- NO "3 feature cards in a row"
- NO bento grids
- NO 3 pricing tiers
- NO terminal window mockups
- NO check mark bullets
- NO dashes

#### Icons & Typography
- NO Lucide icons
- NO sparkle icons
- NO animated arrows
- NO Geist or Space Grotesk fonts

#### UX & Content
- NO fake testimonials
- NO "It's not X, it's Y" copywriting frameworks
- NO skeleton loaders
- NO over-animations (keep it precise and purposeful)
